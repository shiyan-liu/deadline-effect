# Data and output terms

OpenReview's [Terms of Use](https://openreview.net/legal/terms), checked 7 November 2026, distinguish review/metareview Comments (CC BY 4.0), associated metadata (CC0), and submitted article PDFs (their separately indicated licences). Record access controls remain applicable independently of licensing.

Released numeric rating, timestamp, decision and provenance fields retain their source metadata dedication. Original numerical text features, model-output labels, aggregate summaries and result reports are made available by Shiyan Liu under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), to the extent any original rights subsist. This does not override source rights or warrant that article PDFs have the same licence.

Attribution: OpenReview; the ICLR 2024, ICLR 2025, ICLR 2026 and NeurIPS 2025 organisers; the anonymous reviewers and area chairs who contributed the records; and Shiyan Liu for the derived numerical analyses and annotations. Preserve source forum/review IDs and cite the paper when using these artifacts.

The package excludes full review/metareview prose, source article PDFs, personal contact information, and private discussion exports. If separately retrieving and redistributing source text, follow its attribution requirements and readability restrictions.
